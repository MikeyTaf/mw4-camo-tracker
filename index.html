MW4 Camo Tracker

Divergence

Harmonic Break challenges appear once all 33 launch weapons have Platinum. Finish all 33 Harmonic Break challenges and Divergence unlocks, which opens the first Mastery camo in each mode.

Mode Mastery

Each mode has its own three-camo track, unlocked in order after Divergence. These count with any weapon or mix of weapons.

Weapon Prestige

A separate optional track per weapon once it reaches max level. It doesn't block anything above. Tick these for the weapon you're prestiging.

Challenge text is the Multiplayer version unless marked. DMZ counts AI kills with higher targets; Warzone usually halves the MP target. DMZ and Warzone versions are shown where a source published them. Weapon and Special progress is shared across all three modes.
Season 1 guns (Cronen Osprey, CSG-35) are tracked but not counted toward the 33-weapon gates, since Activision hasn't said whether they count. The JAK 12 and ZLR Talonhawk are left out until their challenges are published. A few published lines look odd (the VEL 46 has two prone-kill specials); they're shown as listed, so check the in-game text if one doesn't match.
Sources: Game Rant (military levels and specials), CODMunity (DMZ/Warzone versions), MetaRadar (Platinum and Harmonic Break by class), Android Experto (Activision's ladder and Sang 9mm examples), LFCarry (Warzone Mastery).
